Question:
Which loop gives you access to the index?
Why might that matter?

Answer:
The traditional for loop gives you access to the index. 
This matters because you can use the index to access or change a specific item in 
the ArrayList.

Question:
What happens internally when removing from the middle?

Answer:
When removing from the middle of an ArrayList, the elements after it shift one 
position to the left to fill the empty space.

Question:
Why might ArrayList become inefficient for large data sets?

Answer:
ArrayList can become inefficient because searching takes longer with more students, and 
removing from the middle requires shifting other elements.
